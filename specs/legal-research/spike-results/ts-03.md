## Result

First-class persisted citation anchors are feasible and selected. The application can consume TS-02 passage identities, persist structured claims and multiple evidence relationships, reject invented or stale evidence, reopen citations after restart, and navigate by stored page coordinates without parsing model footnotes or searching for quotation text.

## Production limitations

This is an isolated vertical prototype, not yet wired into the main OpenCode session UI or matter authorization. It uses synthetic fixtures and a simulated structured model result. Production integration must add matter scoping, centralized tool interception, export, and the composed ChatGPT → Docling → citation gate. Legal treatment remains deliberately unevaluated and is not inferred from source resolution.

## Decision

Adopt first-class citation anchors and the explicit finalization transaction. Do not promote provisional markers, accept model-authored footnote identity, or introduce quote-search navigation as an invisible fallback.
