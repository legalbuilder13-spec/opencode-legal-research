# TS-02 result: Docling OCR and visual provenance

Status: conditionally selected; corpus gates remain
Date: 2026-08-23
Worker: [`../../../packages/legal-evidence-worker`](../../../packages/legal-evidence-worker)

## Result

The core PDF contract is feasible. Docling plus portable Tesseract OCR can produce exact model-visible text, correct page associations, canonical page images, and stable top-left regions for native, scanned, and mixed PDFs. The architecture is conditionally selected pending the full fixture corpus and source-format matrix.
