## Release blockers

The fork is not ready to call the PRD complete or to market as a Harvey/Legora replacement. The blocking work is:

1. pass the supervised strict-visual renderer's reviewed live-web security/OCR corpus on every supported platform;
2. run live CourtListener acquisition with an authorized token;
3. assemble and attorney-adjudicate the full document/question/adversarial corpus and meet its thresholds;
4. complete worker sandboxing, malformed-input security tests, and organizational ChatGPT workspace policy;
5. smoke-test signed desktop installers, renderer, and OCR worker on every supported platform; complete the dependency/model license audit and reviewed localization; and run the full Electron window-to-answer restart gate. The Bun workbench companion, Electron renderer, and relocatable offline OCR resource are now built and supervised.
